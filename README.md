> **Makkal Nalan AI** is a bilingual (Tamil & English) citizen welfare navigator and eligibility matching application for Android and Web. It empowers everyday citizens, students, farmers, women, and senior citizens to discover government welfare schemes, understand eligibility criteria in simple language, check required documents, and apply with confidence.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🌐 **Bilingual (தமிழ் & English)**: Complete interface and database accessible in both Tamil and English with 1-tap switching.
- 🎯 **4-Question Instant Eligibility Checker**: Answer Age, Occupation, Annual Income, and State to instantly see all matching schemes.
- 📚 **Expanded 18-Scheme Catalog**: Covers Students, Women, Farmers, Seniors, Health, Housing, Traditional Artisans, and Differently Abled citizens.
- ⭐ **Saved & Pinned Schemes**: Bookmark schemes with 1-tap to easily present to e-Sevai operators or family members.
- 🖨️ **Printable e-Sevai Slip**: Generate and print clean application slips with verified document checklists and official URLs.
- 📋 **Interactive Document Readiness Checklist**: Track essential certificates (Aadhaar, Ration Card, Income, Community, Nativity) with progress saving.
- 🔊 **Voice Reader / Text-to-Speech**: Integrated audio reader in Tamil (`ta-IN`) and English (`en-IN`) for elderly citizens.
- 📲 **1-Tap WhatsApp Share**: Share scheme details and official apply links directly to family and community groups.
- 🏢 **e-Sevai & Help Centers**: Direct guidance to local CSC/e-Sevai touchpoints and CM Helpline `1100`.
- 📱 **Native Android Target SDK 35**: Edge-to-edge transparent system bars, window insets, predictive back navigation, and offline PWA service worker.

## 📱 Building & Installation

### 1. Android APK (`MakkalNalan.apk`)

`MakkalNalan.apk` is compiled using Android SDK Build-Tools, D8 DEX compiler, 4-byte ZipAlignment, and `apksigner` with V1, V2, and V3 signatures for universal compatibility across Android 7.0 to Android 15+.

- **Download APK**: [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/apravint/makkal-nalan-ai/releases)

### 2. Live Web Progressive Web App (PWA)

Use the app instantly in any mobile or desktop browser:
👉 **[https://apravint.github.io/makkal-nalan-ai/](https://apravint.github.io/makkal-nalan-ai/)**

- Fully installable to your Android/iOS home screen.
- 100% offline access via integrated Service Worker (`sw.js`).
