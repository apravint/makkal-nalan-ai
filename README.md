# Makkal Nalan AI - Citizen Welfare & Scheme Navigator 🏛️🇮🇳 (மக்கள் நலன்)

> **Makkal Nalan AI** is a bilingual (Tamil & English) citizen welfare navigator and eligibility matching application for Android and Web. It empowers everyday people, students, farmers, women, and senior citizens to discover government welfare schemes, understand eligibility criteria in simple language, check required documents, and apply with confidence.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🌐 **Bilingual (தமிழ் & English)**: Complete interface and database accessible in both Tamil and English with 1-tap switching.
- 🎯 **4-Question Instant Eligibility Checker**: Answer Age, Occupation, Annual Income, and State to instantly see all matching schemes.
- 📋 **Interactive Document Readiness Checklist**: Track essential certificates (Aadhaar, Ration Card, Income, Community, Nativity) with progress saving.
- 🔊 **Voice Reader / Text-to-Speech**: Integrated audio reader in Tamil (`ta-IN`) and English (`en-IN`) for elderly citizens.
- 📲 **1-Tap WhatsApp Share**: Share scheme details and official apply links directly to family and community groups.
- 🏢 **e-Sevai & Help Centers**: Direct guidance to local CSC/e-Sevai touchpoints and CM Helpline `1100`.
- 📱 **Native Android Integration**: Built with hardware acceleration, tactile haptic feedback, and offline persistence.

## 📱 Building & Installation

### Android APK (`MakkalNalan.apk`)

`MakkalNalan.apk` is compiled using Android SDK Build-Tools 34, D8 DEX compiler, 4-byte ZipAlignment, and `apksigner` with V1, V2, and V3 signatures for universal compatibility across Android 7.0 to Android 15+.

Download the latest APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/apravint/makkal-nalan-ai/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
